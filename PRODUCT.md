# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring managers, recruiters, and anyone evaluating Humberto Axl for a software development role. They need to quickly understand his experience, technical range, working style, and likely fit for their team.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Humberto as a capable, production-minded developer and gives prospective employers enough credible evidence to consider him for development roles. Success means a visitor leaves confident in his ability and can readily imagine him contributing to their team.

## Positioning

Humberto is a full-stack developer with more than five years of experience across public- and private-sector web applications. His profile combines frontend and backend breadth with an emphasis on clean architecture, intuitive interfaces, code quality, collaboration, and software that works reliably in production.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will review the site quickly as part of sourcing or candidate evaluation. The home page introduces Humberto, summarizes his professional experience and skills, and provides additional personal context. A dedicated `/resume` route supports deeper evaluation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve factual employment history, experience duration, skills, and professional claims unless Humberto supplies updated information.
- Keep the portfolio suitable for a broad range of software development opportunities rather than narrowing it to one job title or industry.
- The implementation is a responsive React 19, TypeScript, Vite, and Material UI web application.
- Existing public-facing terminology includes “Full Stack Developer,” “Experience,” “Skills,” and “About Me.”
- Do not fabricate employers, projects, metrics, testimonials, qualifications, or technology experience.

## Brand Commitments

- The personal name is Humberto Axl and the current professional title is Full Stack Developer.
- The site should have a recognizable identity and must not feel like a generic developer portfolio template.
- Space, stars, and the universe are a preferred source of character, but they are flexible rather than mandatory; a stronger distinctive direction may replace them if it serves the portfolio better.
- The voice should feel credible, direct, capable, and human rather than inflated or overly corporate.

## Evidence on Hand

- A real profile photograph is available at `src/assets/profile-pic.jpg`.
- Professional experience content is implemented in `src/components/ui/Timeline.tsx`.
- Technical capabilities are listed in `src/components/Skills.tsx`.
- Current biography and experience claims are in `src/components/AboutMe.tsx` and `src/components/Hero.tsx`.
- The application includes a résumé page at `/resume`.
- No testimonials, case studies, client logos, or independently verified performance metrics are currently present; future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with credible evidence that helps an employer assess capability and fit quickly.
2. Show breadth without becoming vague: connect technical range to real production-minded practice.
3. Make personality and identity memorable while keeping professional content easy to scan.
4. Prefer honest, specific language over generic developer claims or unsupported hype.
5. Make the next step for an interested employer obvious and low-friction.
